Why Dakhla is a kiter's paradise

Perfect Flat Lagoon to world class waves

From the Main Lagoon, the Speed Spot and the Secret Spot to the waves of Lassarga and la Pointe d'Or you get butter flat water and long running waves. Ideal conditions for oldschool, big air new old school, and proper wave riding.

April brings 17-28 knots of steady wind, with 90% windy days. The offshore NE/N wind creates perfect conditions for all disciplines.
