Frequently Asked Questions

What is the deposit amount?

To confirm your registration for our kite & yoga retreats, a deposit covering the accommodation cost + 190€ for the activites is required at the time of booking (ex. Kashkazi in Kenya 790€ deposit). The remaining balance must be paid no later than 1 months before the start of the coaching. Your spot will be fully confirmed once the deposit has been received.

What's your cancellation policy?

• Cancellations made more than 60 days before the start of the retreat: full refund of the balance.
• Cancellations made between 60 and 30 days before the start: 50% of the total amount will be refunded.
• Cancellations made less than 30 days before the start: no refund will be issued.
In case of cancellation from our side (e.g., due to unforeseen circumstances), all payments including the deposit will be fully refunded.

Do you offer insurance?

We recommend all participants obtain travel insurance that covers adventure sports - and medical repatriation

What equipment do I need to bring?

A detailed packing list will be provided after booking. Kite equipment rental is available if needed with partner.

Can I book a private trip?

Yes, we offer private customized coaching. Please select reach out to this link and give us detail of your plan or ask where we are in the world at the moment.
